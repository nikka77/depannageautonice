What we do, and what we don't.

Six kinds of call-out cover most of the calls we receive. In every case, the diagnosis is made on the spot and the price is given before any work starts.

/01

Towing & transport

Your vehicle collected and taken safely to our workshop or wherever you choose.

All light vehicles · whole Alpes-Maritimes

/02

Roadside repair

Quick diagnosis and repair on the spot to get you back on the road.

Back on the road straight away when possible

/03

Wrong fuel

Put the wrong fuel in? We drain the tank and get you going again without damaging the engine.

Do not start the engine — call first

/04

Tyre change

Puncture or damaged tyre? Quick replacement on the spot, all vehicles.

Spare wheel, repair kit or towing

/05

Battery & starting

Flat battery or won't start — diagnosis and replacement on the spot if needed.

Charge test before any replacement

/06

Engine overheating

Engine overheating? Pull over and call us — safe intervention, immediate diagnosis.

Switch off, do not open the coolant tank

Also available

Keys, motorbikes, EVs, scrap cars and more

Keys locked in or lost, locking wheel nut, underground car parks, motorbikes and scooters, vans and motorhomes, electric and hybrid cars, long-distance transport to Monaco and Italy, free scrap car removal, secure storage. Every price is published.

What we don't handle

  • Lorries and vehicles over 3.5 t: on quotation, we confirm feasibility when you call.
  • Motorways and expressways: recovery must be carried out by the operator approved for that network.
